# Arin Vale — Character Bible

## Identity lock
Arin Vale is the original protagonist of HUNTER 2.0. Across all 20 chapters, preserve:
- Face shape, eye shape, nose, mouth, and skin tone
- Black, tousled hairstyle and recognizable hair silhouette
- Height reference: 187 cm
- Lean athletic body proportions
- Core black/charcoal visual identity with electric-blue energy accents

Do not redesign his identity between stages. Progression changes equipment, materials, weaponry, aura intensity, and unlocked abilities.

## Five progression stages
1. **Awakening (E-rank):** basic dark tactical outfit, starter sword, faint blue energy.
2. **Evolved (D/C-rank):** reinforced shoulders and forearms, brighter blue accents, energy dash.
3. **Elite (B-rank):** black-silver armor, upgraded blade, stronger combat effects.
4. **Legendary (A-rank):** upgraded long coat, violet accents, shadow-themed abilities.
5. **Ultimate Awakening (S-rank):** final black-silver armor, controlled blue-violet aura, evolved weapons and ultimate transformation.

## Animation checklist
Idle, walk, run, sprint, start/stop locomotion, jump start/loop/land, dodge forward/back/left/right, sword light combo, heavy attack, hit reactions, stagger, death, and awakening cinematic.

## Production notes
The generated concept art is a visual guide, not a rigged 3D asset. Model and texture the character in Blender, create a humanoid rig, author/retarget animations, export FBX, and import into Unreal Engine. Keep the base body and head stable; use modular armor meshes or controlled material/equipment swaps for the five stages.
